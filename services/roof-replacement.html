What does a roof replacement in Plano include?

A full roof replacement in Plano means stripping the old shingles off, inspecting and repairing the decking underneath, and rebuilding the roof with new underlayment, flashing, and shingles. It is the whole system, not a patch.

Here is what a proper tear-off covers:

  • A full tear-off down to the wood decking, so nobody roofs over old problems.
  • Decking inspection and repair for any soft or rotted boards we find once the old roof is off.
  • New underlayment across the whole deck, the water barrier under your shingles.
  • New flashing around chimneys, vents, and valleys, where most future leaks would otherwise start.
  • New shingles, ridge caps, and ventilation built back to current standards.
  • A magnetic nail sweep across your yard and driveway before we leave.

A tear-off costs more than a patch and takes longer, and that is exactly why we do not recommend one unless the roof has earned it.

How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

If the roof is under 15 years old with damage in one spot, it is usually a repair. If it is past its life or the damage runs across several sections, a replacement is usually the smarter money.

We tell Plano homeowners to weigh these:

  • Age. Most asphalt roofs in our climate last 15 to 25 years. Two Texas summers age a roof faster than one winter up north.
  • Widespread wear. Curling, balding, and granule loss across the whole roof, not one slope, points to replacement.
  • Damage in multiple areas. A few storm-struck shingles is a repair. Damage across several sections usually is not.
  • Repeat leaks. If you have patched the same roof three times, the roof is telling you something.
  • Sagging. A dip in the roofline is a structural issue, not a shingle issue, and needs a look right away.

About half our inspections end with us saying the roof is fine and does not need replacing. That is a terrible sales strategy, and we do it anyway. If a repair will genuinely get you a few more good years, we will point you to our roof repair side instead.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most Plano homes are a one to two day job once we start, weather permitting. Bigger or steeper roofs run longer, and North Texas storms can push a start date.

A typical single-family tear-off and rebuild is a day, sometimes two. We stage the materials, strip the old roof, repair any decking, and build the new system back in one clean pass so your home is not left open to the weather. If rain is in the forecast we plan around it rather than race it, because a roof rushed ahead of a storm is a roof done twice.
