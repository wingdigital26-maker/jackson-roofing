What does hail damage do to a roof?

Hail bruises and cracks shingles, knocks the protective granules loose, and dents the soft metal on vents and flashing. A lot of it you cannot see from the driveway, which is exactly why it gets ignored until it leaks.

Here is what we look for after a storm in Plano:

  • Bruised or cracked shingles, soft spots where the hail struck and weakened the mat underneath.
  • Granule loss, bald patches where the hail knocked the grit off and exposed the shingle to the sun.
  • Dented vents, flashing, and ridge caps, the soft metal that dents first and leaks later.
  • Cracked or split shingles from larger stones, an open door for water.
  • Bruising you cannot see from the ground, which is why a real look means getting on the roof.

The trouble with hail is timing. A bruised shingle can look fine from the street for months, then let water into the decking the first hard rain after the storm. Catching it now is a repair. Finding it after the leak is a bigger repair.

How do I know if my roof has hail damage?

Some signs you can spot from the ground, dented gutters and downspouts, grit collected at the base of the downspout, dinged window screens or AC fins. But the roof itself needs a closer look, because the worst hail bruising hides in plain sight.

What to check after a storm:

  • Dented gutters, downspouts, and metal fascia. If the hail dented your gutters, it hit your roof too.
  • Granules in the gutters or at the downspout. A fresh pile of grit after a storm points to shingle damage.
  • Dinged window screens, AC fins, or fence tops. Soft metal around the house records the size of the stones.
  • Spatter marks. Clean spots on a dirty surface show where hail struck.
  • A ceiling or attic stain after the next rain. By then water is already getting in, so do not wait for it.

What should I do right after a hailstorm?

Stay off the roof, look for interior signs first, and get a professional inspection while the damage is fresh. A ceiling stain or a drip means water is already in, so put a bucket under it and call.

From the ground, do a quick walk around the house for dented gutters, spatter marks, and grit at the downspout. Do not climb up to check yourself, wet or hail-struck roofs are slick and steep is unforgiving. Then have a roofer get on the roof and document what the storm did with photos, so you have a clear record of the damage and a written quote for the fix. Frequently asked questions

Is hail damage always visible from the ground?

No. Dented gutters and grit at the downspout are ground-level clues, but the worst shingle bruising hides on the roof surface and needs a closer look. That is why we get on the roof and photograph it.

How soon should I get my roof checked after hail?

Sooner is better. A bruised shingle can look fine for weeks, then leak the first hard rain after the storm. A free inspection while the weather is calm beats finding the damage after water is inside.

Do you offer free storm damage inspections in Plano?

Yes. Inspections and estimates are free with no obligation. If the storm barely touched your roof, we will say so.

Can hail damage be repaired, or do I need a new roof?

It depends on how widespread the damage is. Scattered damage in one area is usually a repair. Bruising and granule loss across the whole roof often means replacement. We give you the honest call after we look.
