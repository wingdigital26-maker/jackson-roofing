What does roof repair in Frisco usually involve?

Most Frisco repairs come down to shingles, flashing, or sealing, not the whole roof. Replacing a handful of shingles the wind lifted, resealing flashing that dried out in our heat, securing a loose ridge cap, or tracing and fixing a single leak are the everyday jobs.

Frisco grew fast, so we work on a real mix here. What are the signs my Frisco roof needs repair?

The clearest signs are things you can spot from the ground: missing or curling shingles, dark bald patches, grit in the gutters, or a stain on an interior ceiling. Any one of those is worth a closer look.

Here is what we tell Frisco homeowners to watch for:

  • Shingles that are missing, cracked, or curling. Visible from the driveway, and an open door for water.
  • Granules in the gutters or at the downspout. A shingle sheds its protective grit as it ages. A lot of grit means the surface is wearing thin.
  • Flashing that looks loose or rusted. The metal around chimneys, vents, and valleys is where most leaks actually start, not the open field of the roof.
  • A ceiling or attic stain. Even a small brown ring means water is getting past the roof somewhere, and it gets worse in the next storm.
  • A lifted or cracked ridge cap. The shingles along the peak take the most wind, and they loosen first.

Can my roof be repaired instead of replaced?

Often, yes, and we will tell you honestly when it can. A roof under 15 years old with an isolated problem, a leak in one valley or a patch of storm-struck shingles, is usually a repair, not a replacement.

We do not push a new roof on someone who needs a five-shingle fix. About half our inspections end with us saying the roof is fine, which is a terrible sales strategy, and we do it anyway.
