Roof Replacement in Frisco TX: Tear Off to Cleanup

Tear off to the deck, rebuilt in a day on most Frisco homes, quoted in writing before anything comes off.

A roof replacement is the kind of purchase most people make once or twice in a lifetime, which is exactly why the process should be boring and predictable. Roof replacement in Frisco TX means taking the old roof off down to the decking, repairing what is underneath, and building the whole system back: underlayment, flashing, shingles, ridge. We are Jackson Roofing, a family business on North Texas roofs since 2000, and on most Frisco homes we do that in a single day with our own crews.

When does a Frisco roof actually need replacing?

When the damage is spread across several slopes, the decking underneath has softened, or the roof is past its useful life and problems keep coming back in different places.

Frisco is a young city with an old roofing problem. The neighborhoods that filled in around Stonebriar and along Legacy Drive through the early 2000s are now hitting twenty years, and builder-grade three-tab shingles do not get past that mark gracefully here. The sections west of the Dallas North Tollway are a few years behind them and only starting to complain. Wide open subdivisions with young trees do not help either, because there is nothing between an exposed west slope and a July afternoon. The pattern we see is consistent: granules wearing off the south and west slopes first, then shingles going brittle, then a hail season that cracks what was already tired.

If the trouble is in one place and the rest of the roof is sound, we will tell you that and quote a roof repair instead. About half our inspections end with the homeowner keeping their money in their pocket. That is not a slogan, it is what happens when you inspect honestly.

How long does a roof replacement take in Frisco?

One day for most single family homes here, from the first truck in the driveway to the final cleanup. Larger houses, steep pitches, and cut-up rooflines with many valleys can run to a second day.

The day runs in a predictable order. Trucks and a dumpster arrive early and the ground gets protected, including the flower beds, the patio, and the AC units. Then tear off, one section at a time so no more roof is open than we can close before evening. Once the deck is bare we inspect it and replace any plywood that has softened, which is the one line item nobody can price exactly in advance. Then dry in with underlayment, new flashing at every chimney, valley, and pipe boot, shingles from the eaves up, and ridge caps last.

Cleanup ends with a magnetic nail sweep across the yard, the driveway, and the street. We run the magnet whether or not anyone is home, because a nail in a tire next week is still our fault.

What does a roof replacement in Frisco include?

The whole system, not just the shingles. Anyone quoting you shingles alone is quoting half a roof.

  • Tear off to the deck. Old shingles and felt removed, not layered over.
  • Decking repair. Soft or rotten plywood cut out and replaced, quoted per sheet so you see exactly what changed and why.
  • Underlayment. The waterproof layer that does the real work while the shingles take the weather.
  • New flashing. Chimneys, valleys, skylights, and pipe boots. This is where most roofs leak, and reusing old flashing to save a little is how a new roof leaks in year two.
  • Ventilation check. Intake at the eaves and exhaust at the ridge. A roof with a blocked attic cooks itself from below and ages years early.
  • Shingles and ridge. Your choice of grade, installed by our crews rather than a subcontracted team we have never met.
  • Cleanup and magnetic sweep. Debris out the same day.

What shingle grade makes sense in Frisco?

For most Frisco homes, architectural shingles are the sensible baseline and Class 4 impact-rated shingles are the upgrade worth considering.

Frisco sits in the same hail belt as the rest of Collin County, which takes damaging hail most springs. Standard three-tab shingles are the cheapest way to put a roof on and the fastest way to be back up there after a storm. Architectural shingles are heavier and hold up better. Class 4 impact-rated shingles are tested by dropping a steel ball twice in the same spot and only pass if the mat does not crack, and on a house you plan to keep they usually earn the difference back.

We do not push the top of the range. If you are selling in two years, we will say the upgrade probably will not pay you back. Whichever grade you pick goes on the written quote with the line items visible before anything comes off the roof.

What happens if a storm damaged the roof?

We inspect for storm damage specifically, because hail damage is often invisible from the ground and it changes the decision.

Bruising, granule loss in patches, cracked mats, and dented soft metal all tell us how much life the roof has left, and sometimes a roof that looked fine from the driveway turns out to be finished. Our storm and hail damage repair page covers what we look for. After a big hail event across Frisco and Collin County, every honest roofer in the area is booked for weeks, so if water is actively coming in, say so when you call and we will move you up and get a temporary cover on it.
