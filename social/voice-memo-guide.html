The 60-second voice memo

You do not have to be on camera. Your real voice telling a real job story, over footage of that job, is the content that actually gets people to call. One memo becomes a finished reel.

1 How simple it is

  • Open the Voice Memos app on your phone.
  • Pick ONE recent job and talk about it for about a minute, like you are telling a buddy.
  • Grab 3 or 4 photos or short clips of that same job (see the shot list).
  • Text the memo and the photos to the office. That is it. We turn it into the reel.

2 What to say (do not script it, just hit these beats)

Talk it through in order. If you stumble, keep going, we edit it.

1. Where and why.
"This one is in west Plano. Homeowner called us out after that May hail."
2. What you found up there.
"Got on the roof and the whole north slope was bruised up, stuff you cannot see from the ground."
3. Why it matters.
"That knocks the granules off and ages the roof way faster than it should."
4. What you did about it.
"We documented all of it, walked her through the photos, and got her a full replacement."
5. The nudge.
"If a storm came through your street, do not wait for a leak. We will take a look for free."

3 Five tips that make it land

  • One job per memo. Keep it to a single story.
  • Name the city or neighborhood. Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen. Local is the whole point.
  • Talk like a person, not an ad. The rougher and realer, the better it works.
  • 45 to 75 seconds is the sweet spot. Shorter is fine.
  • Quiet-ish spot if you can, the truck is perfect. We clean up the audio.

4 What happens next

  • Your voice becomes the reel. We lay it over your job photos and clips.
  • We add big captions on screen (most people watch with the sound off).
  • You get a finished vertical reel back, ready to post to Instagram, Facebook, and the rest.
One 60-second memo a week is enough. That alone gives us a steady stream of real, personal reels that sound like you, not a robot and not a stock ad.
