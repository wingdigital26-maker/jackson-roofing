Questions people actually ask us

How much does a roof replacement cost?
It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the material you pick, and what we find under the old shingles, so we don't quote numbers over the phone. The free inspection gets you a written estimate for your actual roof, and it doesn't obligate you to anything.
Will my insurance cover hail damage?
Often, but it depends on your policy and what the adjuster finds. We photograph the damage during the inspection so you have documentation, and if a claim makes sense we'll help you through the process with your insurance company.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential replacements our crews do take a day or two. Bigger or more complicated roofs can take longer, and we'll tell you up front if yours will.
Do you charge for the inspection?
No. We come out, look at the roof, take photos, and give you a straight answer for free. About half the time that answer is that your roof is fine.
What areas do you cover?
Plano and the surrounding North Texas towns. If you're not sure whether we come out your way, send us a message and ask.

Worried about your roof? Stop guessing.

Send us a message and we'll come take a look. It's free, and about half the time we end up telling people their roof is fine.
