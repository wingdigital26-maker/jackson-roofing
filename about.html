Our story

Quality work, and people who stick around

Since 2000, Jackson Roofing has been the go-to roofing company for North Texas. What started as a local roofer has grown into a trusted name, built on quality workmanship and personalized care rather than hard selling.

Owner Chris Jackson has a simple reputation in this community: he turns clients into friends. That comes from being personally involved in the work, treating every home like it matters, and telling homeowners the truth about their roof even when the answer is that the roof is fine.

From the first free inspection to the final cleanup, our aim is the same. Do the job right, keep it honest, and leave homeowners glad they called.
