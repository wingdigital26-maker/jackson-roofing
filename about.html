Two decades of the same approach

Chris Jackson founded Jackson Roofing in 2000, and it has been a family-run roofing company ever since. The business grew the slow way, one referred neighbor at a time, never with a hard sell.

The approach hasn't changed in all that time: tell homeowners the truth about their roof, including when the truth is that it doesn't need anything, and do the work with our own certified crews. That is why customers keep calling back and sending their neighbors.

How we run jobs

Our crews are certified installers, and we don't cut corners on materials. The company is fully insured, which protects you as much as it protects us. Every estimate is written down and free, and if a hailstorm caused the damage, we'll help you deal with the insurance claim from start to finish.

None of that is a slogan. It's just how Chris has always run the company, and it's why customers stick around.
