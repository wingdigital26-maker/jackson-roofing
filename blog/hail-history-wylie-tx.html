What the Hail Record Shows for Wylie Roofs

By Chris Jackson, Owner · September 8, 2026

We pulled the NOAA storm data for Wylie and the surrounding counties. The numbers tell us how often hail falls and how big the stones get. That history guides what we look for on a roof after a storm.

How often does hail hit a roof in Wylie TX?

The NOAA Storm Events Database lists 137 hail events in Collin, Denton, Dallas, Rockwall and Kaufman counties over the last 24 months. Of those, 122 were one inch or larger. The events occurred on 27 distinct storm days. Spread over two years that is a hail day somewhere in our service area a little more than once a month on average, not once a month in any one town. The data covers June 2024 through May 2026. The database publishes on a lag so the most recent month in the archive is May 2026. We do not have data for June through August 2026 yet.

Wylie itself recorded three hail reports on March 25 2025. A trained spotter noted ping pong ball sized hail and estimated winds near 60 mph. The fire department at Wylie Fire Station 1 reported hail up to ping pong ball size that accumulated four to six inches deep. An amateur radio operator logged one inch hail at the Park and Country Club. Those reports give us a concrete picture of what a Wylie homeowner can expect when hail falls.

What size hail has hit Wylie in recent years?

The specific Wylie reports show hail ranging from one inch to 1.5 inches. On March 25 2025 we saw two reports of 1.5 inch hail and one report of one inch hail. The next recorded event in the area was on April 27 2026 with 1.25 inch hail in Saint Paul, a nearby community. One inch is about a quarter and 1.5 inches is about a ping pong ball, which is what the spotters actually called it. That is below golf ball size, and it is squarely in the range that bruises asphalt shingles without tearing them off.

The broader county data shows larger stones as well. The largest hail in the 24 month window was a three inch stone on April 25 2026 near Lakeview in Dallas County. Collin County also saw 2.25 inch hail near Parker on that same March 25 2025 day, and two inch stones at Melissa, Forest Grove, Lebanon and Shepton on other dates. While those larger stones have not fallen directly in Wylie, they illustrate the upper end of what the region can experience. If a storm drops stones the size reported for Wylie, we know the impact is enough to dent shingles and create small cracks. What does a 1.5 inch hail stone do to asphalt shingles?

A 1.5 inch stone hits a shingle with enough force to bruise the granule layer. The granules protect the underlying asphalt from UV exposure. When they are knocked loose, the shingle ages faster. The impact can also create a small dent in the fiberglass mat. That dent may not be obvious from the ground but can let water pool under the shingle during rain.

If the dent is deep enough, water can seep behind the shingle and wet the roof deck. Over time that can lead to rot in the decking or corrosion of the nails. The loss of granules also reduces the shingle's ability to reflect heat, which can raise attic temperatures. To keep the roof performing we recommend a visual inspection after any hail event of that size. If you notice dented or missing granules, it may be time for a targeted roof repair or a full replacement if the damage is widespread. What should I check on my roof after a hail storm?

First look at the roof from the ground. Walk around the house and note any visible dents, cracks or missing granules on the shingles. Pay special attention to the areas that face the prevailing wind direction because they take the brunt of the hail. Use a flashlight to see any subtle bruising on the shingle surface.

Stay off the roof. Everything else worth seeing shows up at ground level. Look at the downspout splash blocks and the driveway for loose granules washed off the slopes, check the soft metal around the house for dimples, and look at any painted metal vents. When should I call a professional after hail?

If you see any dented shingles, missing granules, or cracked flashing, call us within a few weeks. Water can find its way behind a damaged shingle quickly once rain returns. Early repair prevents moisture from reaching the decking and saves you from larger repairs later.

Even if the roof looks fine from the ground, somebody on a ladder can see things you cannot. We walk the slopes, photograph what is actually up there, and show you the pictures. Whether a roof needs a small repair or something bigger depends on how widespread the bruising is and how much life the shingles had left before the storm. Frequently asked questions

How many hail events does the NOAA record show for Wylie?

The database shows three reports on March 25 2025 and one report on April 27 2026. Those events involved hail from one inch to 1.5 inches in size.

Can hail smaller than one inch still damage my roof?

Sometimes. Stones under an inch usually bounce off a sound roof without leaving much behind. What matters more is the age of the shingles and whether the hail came in at an angle behind a strong wind. An older roof that has already lost granules has less left to give.

Do I need a roof replacement after a 1.5 inch hail storm?

Not always. If the damage is limited to a few dented shingles, a repair may be enough. A full replacement is considered when many shingles are cracked or the deck is wet.
