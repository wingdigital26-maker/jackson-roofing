How Long Does a Roof Replacement Take in Texas?

By Chris Jackson, Owner · September 7, 2026

Most North Texas homes are torn off and completely re-roofed in one day, usually eight to ten hours from the first truck in the driveway to the last nail sweep.

Bigger homes, steep pitches, and rooflines with a lot of valleys can push into a second day. The paperwork and the wait for a slot usually take longer than the roof does.

We have been replacing roofs in Plano and across North DFW since 2000 with our own crews, so here is the honest timeline rather than the sales version.

How long does a roof replacement take from start to finish?

One day for the roof itself on a typical single family home. One to two weeks from the inspection to the crew arriving, depending on the season.

Break it into three parts and the answer gets clearer:

  • Inspection and quote. Same day in most cases. We go up, take photos, and give you a written quote with itemized line items.
  • Deciding and scheduling. Entirely your pace. We do not do same day pressure, so this part takes however long you want it to take. Once you approve, we book a slot.
  • The work itself. One day for the majority of homes. Two if the house is large, the pitch is steep, or we find decking that has to come off.

The part homeowners underestimate is the middle one, and only because it depends on the weather calendar rather than on us.

What actually happens on the day?

The crew arrives early, protects the ground, strips the old roof to the deck, repairs what needs repairing, and dries the roof in before they leave.

Roughly how it runs:

  1. Around seven in the morning. Trucks and a dumpster arrive. Tarps go over the flower beds, the air conditioning units, and anything on the patio.
  2. Morning. Tear off. This is the loud part. The old shingles and felt come off down to the bare decking, one section at a time so no more roof is open than can be closed that day.
  3. Midday. Deck inspection. Any soft or rotten plywood gets cut out and replaced. This is the step nobody can quote exactly in advance, because you cannot see the deck until the shingles are off.
  4. Afternoon. Dry in and install. Underlayment, then new flashing at the chimneys, valleys, and pipe boots, then shingles from the eaves up, then the ridge caps.
  5. Late afternoon. Cleanup. Debris out, and a magnetic nail sweep across the yard, the driveway, and the street. We run the magnet whether or not anyone is watching, because a nail in a tire a week later is still our fault.

Start to finish that is a long day for the crew and a noisy one for you, but the house is closed up and weather tight the same evening. If you want the detail on how we handle a full re-roof, our roof replacement page goes through the materials and the flashing work.

What slows a roof replacement down?

Four things: rotten decking, steep pitch, complicated rooflines, and rain.

Decking is the common one. Once the old shingles are off, any plywood that has softened from a long running leak has to be cut out and replaced. On a roof that has been leaking quietly for a couple of years, that can add hours. It is also why an honest quote lists decking replacement as a per sheet line item rather than pretending to know the number in advance.

Pitch matters because a crew that has to work roped in moves at maybe half speed. A steep roof on a two story house is a genuinely different job from a low walkable roof on a ranch house.

Roofline complexity adds up quietly. Every valley, dormer, skylight, and pipe penetration is a detail that has to be flashed properly, and details are where roofs leak. A simple gable roof and a cut up roof of the same square footage are not the same day's work.

Then weather. Rain stops a tear off, which is why crews only open what they can dry in before evening. In spring we schedule around the radar constantly. Material choice changes the day too, and our comparison of metal roof versus shingles in Texas covers what each one means for the install.

When is the wait longest?

After hail. Every spring, a hail event across Collin County fills every honest roofer's schedule in the area for weeks.

That is the trade off with using a local company. Out of town crews roll in after a big storm with immediate availability, and some of them are gone by the time the first problem shows up. We have been here since 2000 and we will be here next season, which sometimes means telling you a real date rather than the date you want. If water is actively coming in, tell us when you get in touch and we will move you up and get a temporary cover on it in the meantime.

If the damage turns out to be limited, a targeted roof repair in Plano is often a same week job instead. About half our inspections end with us saying the roof does not need replacing at all, and we would rather tell you that than sell you a day of noise you did not need. If you are not sure which side of the line your roof is on, the ground level signs in our roof maintenance checklist are a decent place to start.

How should you prepare for the day?

Very little is required of you, but a few things make the day easier for everybody.

Move cars out of the driveway before the crew arrives so the dumpster and the trucks have room. Take pictures and mirrors off the walls upstairs, because tear off vibration will knock things crooked. Put anything fragile in the attic somewhere safe, since dust and small debris come down between the boards. If you have pets that hate noise, arrange somewhere quiet for them to spend the day.

You do not need to leave the house, and you do not need to be home either. Plenty of our customers go to work and come back to a finished roof and a clean yard.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a roof replacement take on an average house?

One day for most single family homes in North Texas, roughly eight to ten hours. Large homes, steep pitches, and complex rooflines can run into a second day.

Do I need to leave the house during a roof replacement?

No. You can stay. It is loud and the vibration will knock a few pictures crooked, but there is no reason to move out for a one day job. Pets are usually happier somewhere quiet.

What slows a roof replacement down?

Rotten decking found after tear off, steep pitch, complicated rooflines with many valleys and penetrations, and rain.

How long from signing to the crew showing up?

Usually days rather than weeks in normal conditions. After a major hail event the whole area is booked out. Active leaks get prioritized, so mention it when you get in touch.
